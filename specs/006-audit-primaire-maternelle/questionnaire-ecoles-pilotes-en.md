Dear Headteacher,

SCHOLARIS is a school management software. We want it to follow your rules and your habits, not the other way round.

For each question, we write what SCHOLARIS does today. If it is right for your school, tick "Yes". If not, tick "No" and write how you do it. If you do not know, tick "I don't know".

Only answer the parts that concern your school. Example: a public primary school fills in part 0, part B and part E.

Do not write the name of any pupil, parent or teacher. For sample documents, send a blank copy or hide the names.

All these values can still be changed in SCHOLARIS: your answer sets the starting value.
